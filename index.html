Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about The Cane Shop.

What makes your juice different?

Our juices are cold-pressed daily from certified organic ingredients with zero additives, preservatives, or artificial colors. Every batch is prepared fresh to order in full view of our customers.

Are your ingredients organic?

Yes, we exclusively source from certified organic farms. We believe in quality at every step of the process, from farm to glass.

Do you offer delivery?

Currently, we serve guests at our café in Bodinayakanur. Visit us for the freshest experience or contact us for wholesale inquiries.

Can I customize my order?

Absolutely! Our menu is flexible. We prepare everything fresh to order, so feel free to ask for modifications or combinations.

What are your operating hours?

We're open daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Come visit us anytime for a fresh juice and pastry experience.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes! All our fresh juices are naturally gluten-free. For pastries and baked goods, please ask our staff about gluten-free options available.
